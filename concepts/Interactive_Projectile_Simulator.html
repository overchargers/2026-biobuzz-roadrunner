🔬 What's Happening?

When you launch a projectile, it follows a curved path called a parabola. This happens because:

  • Horizontal motion: No force acts sideways, so it moves at constant speed
  • Vertical motion: Gravity pulls the projectile down, making it accelerate downward
  • Result: The combination creates a curved trajectory!

🎯 Hitting Your Target

To hit a target, you need to consider:

  • Distance: How far away is it?
  • Height: Is it above or below you?
  • Angle: Higher angles = higher arc, lower angles = flatter path
  • Speed: More speed = longer range
